Privacy Policy

JIOVEN · App "Jioven SNS" · Effective 30 October 2026

Who we are

JIOVEN is an independent music label. We publish our own music videos on YouTube, Instagram (@jioven_music), Facebook (Jiovenmusic) and TikTok (@jiovenmusic).

What the Jioven SNS app does

Jioven SNS is an internal tool. It is used only by JIOVEN to post JIOVEN's own videos and captions to JIOVEN's own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to send drafts to JIOVEN's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Information we use

  • The access tokens and account IDs of JIOVEN's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account, needed to publish posts.
  • Basic information about our own posts (post ID, link, view and like counts) to keep a record of what was published.

The app does not collect, store or process personal information about visitors, followers or any other users. It does not read messages, comments or profiles of other people.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on JIOVEN's own computer and are never shared. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to Meta and TikTok in order to publish our posts.

Retention and deletion

Records of our own posts are kept as long as the posts exist. To request deletion of any data related to you, or if you believe the app holds data about you, contact us at the address below. We will reply and delete it within 30 days.
